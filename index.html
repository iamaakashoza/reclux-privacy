Reclux Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 (Reclux 2.0)

On this page

What Reclux handles What Reclux does not do Your permission, every time Why Reclux asks for its permissions Deleting your data Children Changes Contact

Short version: Reclux does not collect, send or sell any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

What Reclux handles

WhatWhere it stays
The picture of your screen, a window or a tab, and the microphone or system sound you choose to record. Edits you make to it (cuts and zooms)Written to your browser's private storage on your own computer, then to the folder you pick when you press Export video
Your webcam, only if you turn on WebcamShown live in a circle on your screen and recorded as part of the screen. Never stored or sent on its own
Where your pointer is on web pages, only if you turn on Cursor zoom and only while recordingKept in memory to suggest zooms in the editor, then thrown away. Not stored, not sent. The web pages themselves are not read
A picture of the tab you are on, taken when you press Start recordingShown behind the share window while the recording starts, then deleted. Not stored, not sent
Your choices (quality, format, microphone, system sound, webcam and cursor zoom on or off)Saved in your browser's local storage on your own computer
Free disk spaceRead while recording, only to show a warning. Not stored, not sent
How loud your microphone is, when the microphone and system sound are both onRead while recording, only to lower the system sound in the file while you speak. Not stored, not sent

What Reclux does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No uploading of recordings or any other data
  • No analytics, ads, tracking or cookies
  • No third-party services
  • No network requests of any kind. It works with the internet switched off

Your permission, every time

Your browser asks you before Reclux can capture the screen, a window, a tab, the microphone or the webcam. Reclux only records while you have started a recording. Access to web pages is asked only when you turn on Webcam or Cursor zoom, never at install, and you can take it back in your browser's extension settings.

Why Reclux asks for its permissions

PermissionReason
downloadsShows the "Save as" window, so you can choose the folder or drive for the finished recording
unlimitedStorageLets a long recording be written to the browser's private storage while you record
scriptingPlaces the webcam circle, the recording controls (when no controls window is open) and cursor tracking on web pages while you record. Removed from the pages when recording stops
activeTabTakes the one picture of the tab you are on, only when you press Start recording
Access to web pages (optional)Asked only when you turn on Webcam or Cursor zoom, so the circle and cursor zoom work on the pages you record
powerKeeps the computer awake while a recording is running (Chrome, Brave, Edge and Opera)

Deleting your data

  • Unsaved recordings: press Discard or Delete in Reclux.
  • Saved files: they are normal files in the folder you chose. Delete them like any other file.
  • Settings: removed when you uninstall Reclux or clear the extension's data.

Children

Reclux is a general tool. It does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
